Returns Policy

We offer a 7-day return window for eligible items under the conditions below.

Return Window

  • Returns must be requested within 7 calendar days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, in original condition, and in original packaging.
  • Proof of order (order ID/email/phone) is required for verification.

Non-Eligible Cases

  • Products with physical damage caused after delivery.
  • Items missing packaging, accessories, or tampered components.
  • Returns requested after the 7-day return period.

Refund Process

After return inspection, approved refunds are processed via the originally agreed method. Processing times depend on payment and courier handling.
